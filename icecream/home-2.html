COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 2-3 weeks ahead for weekend events, and as little as one week for weekday events depending on availability.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely — we have vegan, dairy-free, and gluten-free options available on every package.

How long does setup take?

Our team typically arrives 45-60 minutes before serving time to fully set up and prep the cart.

What areas do you serve?

We serve 12+ cities across the greater bay region — contact us to confirm coverage at your location.

Do you need power on site?

Our carts run on self-contained chilled batteries, so no power outlet is needed for most events.
