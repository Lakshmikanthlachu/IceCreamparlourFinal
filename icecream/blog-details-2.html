10 Dairy-Free Flavors
Richer Than Cream

No dairy, no compromise. Coconut nectar, oat milk emulsions, and fresh fruit sorbets built to taste indulgent, not "diet."

The trick to a rich dairy-free base isn’t a substitute — it’s a completely different fat structure. We blend young coconut cream with a touch of cacao butter, which whips to the same silky mouthfeel as heavy cream without tasting remotely of coconut once other flavors are folded in.

For flavors like our Roasted Almond and Salted Date Caramel, we lean on oat milk instead — it has a naturally rounder, slightly sweet body that pairs beautifully with toasted nuts and doesn’t fight with delicate spice notes.

And our fruit sorbets never needed dairy to begin with — they’re 100% real fruit purée, a touch of cane sugar, and nothing else. Simplicity was always the plant-based option.

Fat Structure, Not Substitution.
