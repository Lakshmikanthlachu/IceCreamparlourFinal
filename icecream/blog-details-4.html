PARTY & CATERING TIPS · AUG 22, 2025

The Ultimate Guide to
Hosting a Gelato Cart Party

From guest count to flavor lineup, here’s exactly how to plan a scoop bar your guests won’t stop talking about.

Start with headcount, not flavors. As a rule of thumb, budget 1.5 scoops per guest for a two-hour event — that accounts for seconds without over-ordering. Our live estimator on the Catering page does this math for you instantly.

Pick 3-4 flavors, not 10. A tight, well-contrasted lineup (one chocolate, one fruit, one nut-based, one wildcard) always outperforms a huge menu — guests decide faster and every tub gets finished instead of half-melted leftovers.

Book your cart 2-3 weeks ahead for weekend events. Our vintage illuminated cart and uniformed scoop captain need lead time to lock in your date, especially during spring and summer wedding season.
