Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead should we book?

We recommend 3–4 weeks in advance, especially for summer weekends. Last-minute bookings are welcome based on cart availability.

Do you travel to venues?

Travel within 20 miles of our flagship parlour is completely free. Beyond 20 miles, a nominal travel fee applies.

Are vegan options available?

Absolutely! We always offer dairy-free fruit sorbets and plant-based oat milk flavors upon request for your event.
