MENU QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you offer sugar-free options?

Yes, we carry two naturally fruit-sweetened, no-added-sugar flavors, available seasonally.

Can I request a custom flavor for a bulk order?

Absolutely — our chefs can craft an exclusive flavor for orders of 50+ guests with two weeks' notice.

How should I store pints at home?

Keep them in the coldest part of your freezer and enjoy within two weeks for the best texture.

Are your cups and spoons compostable?

Yes, all of our serviceware is compostable or made from recycled materials.

Do flavors rotate seasonally?

We launch 4-6 new seasonal flavors every quarter alongside our classic favourites.
